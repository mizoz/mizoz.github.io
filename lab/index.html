How the command bar works

01

A tiny model, trained by me

About 100 KB of int8 weights over hashed word and character features. It runs in your browser in under a millisecond. No server, no API, no tracking.

02

It routes, it doesn't guess

Questions about me are answered from verified facts only. Tool requests open the right tool, already set up ("Instagram story", "YouTube 1080p60").

03

A bigger brain, if you want it

Wake MiniLM-L6, a 23 MB neural search model. It understands what you mean, not just keywords, and it can only answer by quoting verified facts, so it never makes things up.
