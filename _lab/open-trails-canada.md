A lot of people who move to Canada don't know that most of Alberta is public land they can visit, that many parks near Calgary are free, or which passes and licences they need.

Open Trails explains it in plain language, in 14 languages: hiking, camping, Crown land, fishing, rivers and lakes, winter, safety and gear, with a map.

## Details
- Prices and official links live in one file, so every language stays correct.
- Works offline for pages you've already visited.
- An independent community project that always links to the official source.
