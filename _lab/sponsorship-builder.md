Pricing sponsorships is mostly guesswork. This makes the guesswork visible: every tier's value comes from a formula you can see and change.

I built it alongside FirstShow, my sponsorship company.

## Details
- Tick deliverables into Bronze, Silver, Gold and Title tiers, or rename them.
- Value from reach and cost per thousand by channel, plus fixed-value items like booths and tickets.
- Checks for tiers out of order, thin margins and exclusive items sold twice.
- A one-page sponsor sheet that prints to PDF.
