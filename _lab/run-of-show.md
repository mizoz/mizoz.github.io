A run of show is the document everyone at an event reads from: who is on stage, what the AV team does, and when. Most people still build it in a spreadsheet.

Here, start times calculate themselves from durations, a fixed time anchor shows any gap or overlap, and anything that runs past your hard out gets flagged.

## Details
- Templates for a conference day, gala, product launch, wedding reception, live stream show and sponsor activation.
- Print a landscape cue sheet, or a view for just the AV crew or one presenter.
- Live mode for the stage manager's tablet: current segment, countdown, and what's next.
- Works offline, because venue wifi.
