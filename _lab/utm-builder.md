Bad UTM tags quietly wreck your reports. This builder tells you which GA4 channel a link will land in before you send it, and fixes the usual mistakes in one click.

## Details
- Presets for newsletters, LinkedIn, Meta ads, Google Ads, print QR codes, podcasts and sponsors.
- Warnings for capitals, spaces, swapped source and medium, and mediums GA4 files under Unassigned.
- Bulk mode for a whole spreadsheet of links, with CSV export.
- QR codes as PNG or SVG, checked by scanning them back.
