Alberta's outdoor information is scattered across dozens of sites. Alberta Atlas brings it together on one searchable map.

## What's in it
- 689 conservation properties listed for hunting, with published details and restrictions.
- The 2026 pheasant release listings.
- Wildlife management units, public land use zones and Crown-land layers, with a click-to-inspect panel.
- Source and date for everything, plus clear notes on what the data does not prove.

It runs locally for now, and there is no public hosted demo yet.
