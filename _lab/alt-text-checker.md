Alt text is how people using screen readers know what an image shows. Drag the checker to your bookmarks bar and it audits any page you're on.

## Details
- Flags missing, empty, file-name and overlong alt text, and image links with no text.
- Paste HTML instead, or export the report as a CSV.
- A short guide and a helper that walks you through writing good alt text.
