Write once, see it on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Threads, Facebook and Bluesky side by side.

The part people care about is the cut. Each preview shows where "see more" lands on mobile and desktop, so you can check your hook is actually visible.

## Details
- X counting follows X's real weighting rules: links count as 23, some characters count double.
- Emoji are counted the way people see them, not the way computers store them.
- A bold and italic formatter, with a warning that screen readers struggle with styled letters.
- Drafts save in your browser, and a share link carries the post without sending it anywhere.
