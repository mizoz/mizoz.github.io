The rule: if the hardware costs more than about $10, the idea isn't clever enough yet.

## The builds
- **HoldPoint**: a $5 Arduino ballistics calculator. Its numbers are checked against an established ballistics library.
- **Trail Tally**: counts game-trail traffic by the hour for weeks.
- **Trail Finder**: finds game trails in drone photos.
- **Wind Scout**: logs when the morning wind switches (spec).

Each is useful alone. Together they make a scouting kit for less than one trail camera. Builds are labelled honestly with what has and hasn't been tested on real hardware.
