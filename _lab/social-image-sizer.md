Every platform wants a different size, and they keep changing them. I got tired of re-cropping the same photo eight times, so I built this.

Drop in one image and it shows every crop at once: Instagram square, portrait and story, Facebook, LinkedIn post and banner, X, YouTube thumbnail and banner, TikTok, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Google Business and link previews. Click to set a focal point for all of them, or drag and zoom each one on its own.

## What makes it useful
- Red overlays show where each app covers your image with buttons, names or profile photos.
- Export JPG, PNG or WebP, one at a time or all as a zip with sensible file names.
- Sizes checked against each platform's own help pages in October 2026.
- Nothing is uploaded. It all happens in your browser.
